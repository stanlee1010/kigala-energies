Privacy policy

How the Kigala Energies petrol station system uses and protects the information of the people who use it.

Home › Privacy policy

Who this applies to

This system is used only by Kigala Energies staff, station managers and the director to run the petrol stations. It is not a public website and it is not used for marketing.

What information is kept

  • Your account: full name, username, phone and email (if given), your role, the station you work at, your regular and weekly shift, and when you last logged in.
  • Your password: stored only as a one-way scrambled code (a hash). Nobody — not even the manager or the director — can see or recover it; a forgotten password is reset, not looked up.
  • Your work records: pump meter readings, fuel refills and their confirmations, online money, expenses and receipt photos, shift assignments and submissions.
  • The audit log: who did what and when, the previous and the new value, the reason given, and the network (IP) address the action came from.
  • Your browser: one login cookie that keeps you signed in (deleted when you log out or after 12 hours). On the training guide, your ticked lessons are remembered on your own device only. The system uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no Google Analytics.

Why it is kept

  • To run the station: shifts, fuel stock, sales, money and expenses.
  • To keep honest, checkable records of money and fuel (the audit log protects staff as much as the business: it shows exactly what each person entered).
  • To keep the system secure (passwords, login limits, removed accounts).

Who can see it

  • Staff see their own shift, readings, refills, expenses and online money — not other staff's records, not the day's total sales and not the tanks.
  • Managers see the records of the stations they run.
  • The director sees sales and profit for all stations and the list of managers.
  • The information is not sold or shared with anyone outside Kigala Energies, except where the law requires it (for example tax authorities).

How it is protected

Each person has their own login; passwords are hashed; repeated wrong passwords lock the account; each station's records are separated; receipts are checked before they are stored; and online the system uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Backups of the database are kept on the station computer.

How long it is kept

Sales, money, fuel and audit records are kept as long as the business needs them for its accounts and the law (financial records are never deleted, only corrected with a reason). When someone leaves, their account is disabled so they can no longer log in; their past work stays in the records under their name.

Your rights and questions

You can ask your station manager to see the information held about you, to correct your name, phone or email, or to explain any record. Questions the manager cannot answer go to the director of Kigala Energies.

This policy describes how the system works. Kigala Energies should have it reviewed against the data-protection law that applies to it (in Tanzania, the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022) and update the contact details above as needed.
